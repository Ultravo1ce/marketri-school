Panels and pictures

Landscapes, still lifes and ornaments in veneer are the best-known genre of marquetry. Panels decorate living rooms, studies and bedrooms.

Furniture and tabletops

Inlaid designs on tabletops, cabinet doors, chests of drawers and headboards turn furniture into a one-of-a-kind piece.

Boxes and gifts

Boxes, cases and caskets with an inlaid lid make a gift that is kept for generations.

Chessboards and games

Chess and backgammon boards in contrasting woods are a classic where the geometry of the inlay has to be especially precise.

Doors and wall panels

Marquetry in interior finishing: inlaid doors, wall panels, ceiling and parquet insets.

Portraits

Veneer portraits from a photograph are one of the most demanding genres: skin tones and light are conveyed by the choice of species and grain.

Clocks, trays and decor

Wall clocks, trays, frames and other objects that make marquetry part of everyday life.

Student works

Panels made on the marquetry course. Each one is the result of one of the three stages.
