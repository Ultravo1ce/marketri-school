How to get here

The studio is within walking distance of Proletarskaya metro station.

Open the map full size

  1. Leave Proletarskaya metro station by exit 2.
  2. Walk along Abelmanovskaya Street and cross Volgogradsky Avenue at the pedestrian crossing.
  3. Continue along Abelmanovskaya Street past the Puppet Theatre. Right after building 9 bldg 10, turn right.
  4. Walk to Broshevsky Lane and turn left. The studio is at number 4, on the left-hand side.
