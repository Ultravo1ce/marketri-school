Privacy policy

Version of 9 November 2026

This website is for information only and has no forms for entering personal data.

What data is collected

When you visit the site, the Yandex Metrica service automatically receives anonymised technical data: your device and browser type, the pages you opened and your approximate location based on your IP address. Cookies are used for this.

Why

We need this data to understand which materials on the site are useful to visitors and to improve the site. We do not use it to identify you.

How to opt out

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. The site will keep working.

If you contact us

When you call, write in a messenger or send an email, your name and contact details are used only to reply and to book you into a class, and are not shared with third parties.

Website owner

Full name or company name, tax ID and contact details of the website owner.
