What is marquetry?

Marquetry is the art of making inlaid pictures from wood. The image is assembled from thin sheets of veneer of different species: each species has its own colour and grain, and the master chooses them the way a painter chooses paints.

The technique flourished in 17th and 18th century France, where it adorned palace furniture. Today marquetry is a rare craft. What matters in it is patience, a precise knife and a feel for the material, not expensive machinery.

The master’s palette

Marquetry uses no paint: the colour comes from the wood itself. Here are some of the species you will work with on the course.

  • Maple

    Light, almost creamy. The basis for backgrounds and highlights.

  • Birch

    A warm light tone with a soft figure.

  • Oak

    Golden brown, with a pronounced grain.

  • Cherry

    Reddish, it deepens with time.

  • Walnut

    Dark and calm, good for shadows.

  • Wenge

    Almost black, with a striped figure.

Marquetry course

    Getting to know the tools and the material.

    Result: a small ornament in several species

  2. A picture in veneer

    Result: a panel based on a ready-made sketch

  3. Your own work

    The full cycle, from an idea to a finished piece.

    Result: a finished panel or a furniture inset of your own design

More about the course

How a work is made

In class you will go through every stage the master goes through.

  1. Sketch

    The drawing is traced onto tracing paper and divided into pieces.

  2. Choosing the veneer

    A species is chosen for each piece by colour and grain direction.

  3. Cutting

    The pieces are cut out with a knife along the lines of the sketch.

  4. Assembly

    The pieces are fitted together and held with paper tape.

  5. Finishing

    The assembled design is glued onto a backing board, sanded and coated with oil or varnish.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any woodworking experience?

No. The first stage of the course is designed for people who have never worked with veneer. The master demonstrates every technique and helps you at every stage.

Do I need to buy tools and materials?

No, everything you need for the classes is in the studio. If you decide to work at home, the master will tell you which tools to buy.

Can I take just one stage?

The course is built so that each stage relies on the previous one. If you already have some experience, the master will suggest which stage to start from.

How long does the course take?

Please ask the master about the length of the stages and the schedule by phone or in a messenger.

Can I take my work home?

Yes, the works you make in class are yours to keep.

Can I give the course as a gift?

Yes, the course can be given as a gift. Please ask the master for details.

How is marquetry different from intarsia?

In intarsia, pieces are set into recesses cut in solid wood, while in marquetry the whole picture is assembled from thin veneer and glued onto a backing board. Today the two words are often used as synonyms.
