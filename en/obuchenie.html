Marquetry course

The course has three stages. At each one you make a finished work, and by the end of the course, your own panel from your own sketch. All materials and tools are in the studio.

Price
To be confirmed
Format
Classes in the studio
Experience
Not required

Stage 1

The basics

What the stage covers
Getting to know the tools and the material.
What you will learn
  • the tools and veneer species
  • transferring the sketch and marking out
  • cutting with a knife and your first simple design
Result
A small ornament in several species
Duration
To be confirmed

Stage 2

A picture in veneer

What the stage covers
Working with colour, grain and volume.
What you will learn
  • a multicoloured design from a ready-made sketch
  • choosing veneer by colour and grain direction
  • sand shading
Result
A panel based on a ready-made sketch
Duration
To be confirmed

Stage 3

Your own work

What the stage covers
The full cycle, from an idea to a finished piece.
What you will learn
  • preparing your own sketch
  • gluing the design onto a backing board
  • sanding and finishing with oil or varnish
Result
A finished panel or a furniture inset of your own design
Duration
To be confirmed
